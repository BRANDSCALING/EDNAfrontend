      Design notes for this preview (not part of the app)

      1. Sidebar items Home · Make a decision, Decision log, My E-DNA: as decided (17 Nov). Settings at the foot of the sidebar: design choice, because every app offers the settings in BS-BRAND-001 §9.3.
      2. Question wording, Box order, urgent mark and "nothing here changes your result": BS-AI-001 §2, §4, §5 rule 5. Mixed: two orders to choose per decision, BS-AI-001 §2.
      3. Loop figure: BS-BRAND-001 §7.4. Light shades drawn with the register's Pastel 3 (#E6DEEE, #FDE6E7), not §7.4's #E7DFF2 / #FBE0E1, which are outside the 88-colour register: for your decision.
      4. Text sizes 28 / 19 / 13 px as you set for this feature. Primary button Indigo for every person: BS-BRAND-001 §15.1. Panel strip, chips and counters in the person's type colour; Mixed grey: §7.3.
      5. Mentor order and primary rule: BS-BRAND-001 §4.5, §7.2. Approved GIFs used as supplied.
      6. Phone layouts: the web app at phone width and the iOS app use 22 / 15 / 13 (the phone scale in BS-DI-007 §10) instead of 28 / 19 / 13, and the compact loop figure of BS-BRAND-001 §7.4 (captions as bullets, label on the right). A bottom tab bar for Home · Decision log · My E-DNA, and Settings behind the gear, are design choices: BS-DI-007 §0 fixes only the navigation bar, the full-width 52 pt primary button, the 44 pt tap area and the safe area.
      7. Result line and "What to avoid": core types from the BS-AI-001 §4 warnings in plain words; Mixed and leaning from the Workbook 1 commitment lines (BS-DI-009.1 §3) and the BS-AI-001 §4 record. Wording is a design choice for approval.
      8. Leaning people: the leaning order is pre-selected and the other order stays available (BS-DI-009.1 §3, Exercise 3, leaning row); only the leaning loop is drawn, in the light shades (BS-DI-008.2 §3); both mentors stay equal with the leaning side marked "Leads" (BS-BRAND-001 §7.2: a leaning is still Mixed). Grey type colour for every Mixed result: BS-BRAND-001 §7.3.
      9. The flow under the question: Begin with (the chosen card holds Box 1) → Box 2 and Box 3, repeated as many times as the decision needs → End with (the loop that finally validates: the cue in Box 4) → Your decision (Box 4) → Check my decision with the mentor. Boxes, order and contents are BS-AI-001 §2; the repeat is the plus button (§2); the check is the mentor's Box 4 rule and the warnings (§3, §4). Splitting Box 4 into "End with" and "Your decision" is a design choice.
      10. Box placeholders are the mentor's own questions from BS-AI-001 §3, keyed by which mentor leads (the loop the decision begins with). The Decision box carries the mentor's first move (restate as one decision; what it would mean if it worked).
      11. Urgent: the panel "Before you decide" shows the person's Part 1 stress result (BS-AI-001 §4) and their Workbook 1 commitment (BS-DI-009.1 §2) above "End with"; both are example values here. Information colours, BS-BRAND-001 §15.3. Ticked by default in this preview only.
      12. "Save and come back": the Frame can be reopened (BS-AI-001 §5 rule 5); the app records when it was opened, which gives the log its time to decide (BS-DI-009.1 §5). Design choice.
      13. Mixed and leaning people see loop counts on the log tile (began with Logic · began with Feeling · finished in the loop it began with), the loop-finding dashboard of BS-DI-009.1 §3, Exercise 4.
      14. "Help me check a decision I already made": the hand-written log row (decided 17 Nov; BS-DI-009.1 §3 Exercise 4, hand-written rows plain). Same boxes, filled in after the fact; the mentor checks how it was made. A quick row without boxes is on the Decision log screen (Screen 3). Design choice.
      15. "+ Attach document" under every box (it also gives the box more room): not in any OS document. It needs a mentor rule in BS-AI-001 (what the mentor does with a document in a Logic box and in a Feeling box), an attachments table in BS-DATA-002, and a check of BS-WEB-004 (privacy), BS-WEB-008 (AI mentor policy) and BS-WEB-009 (sub-processors) because files go to the AI provider. Design choice for approval.
      16. The two order cards under the question are shown to everyone. Architects and Alchemists have their own order pre-selected; the other card is marked "Your other loop · awareness practice" because Workbook 3 asks them to run one live decision a month the other loop's way, with Box 4 still validated by their own loop (BS-DI-009.3 §2, Exercise 2). That mentor behaviour is not yet in BS-AI-001. Design choice.
      17. Screen 2 (live prototype): the Frame on the left, the mentor on the right. The mentor is given, on every call, the rules of BS-AI-001 §2 to §6 and BS-AI-002 §4 to §6, the person's E-DNA (example values for the preview state), and the state of every box; it answers box by box. It runs on Claude in this preview to prove the behaviour; production runs on the provider named in BS-WEB-008 §4. Nothing is saved.
      18. Credits: one credit = one decision through the Frame to Check (or one existing decision checked), up to 15 mentor turns; the Free tier's decisions are in-app only and not shown on the website (19 Nov). The line, the warning below 20% and the out-of-credits state are design choices; allowances and pack prices are for approval. Terms: BS-WEB-003 §3 (credit allowances shown on the tier page), BS-WEB-006 (refunds).
      19.
