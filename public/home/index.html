Why businesses stall

Three problems, usually mistaken for one.

When growth stops, the advice is always more tactics. The real causes sit underneath: the people running the business, and the order it was built in.

1

The people problem

Partners look at the same numbers and want different things. Instructions do not land with the team. One person carries every decision alone. None of this is about ability. It is about how each person makes a decision, which almost nobody has been shown.

2

The systems problem

Tools chosen one at a time. Processes that live in one person's head. Products launched before the price and the customer were right. Growth that depends on the owner being in the room.

3

The order problem

Scaling before the foundation is profitable, or building assets before the systems that run them, breaks businesses that were working. There is a sequence. Brandscaling builds in it.

Where it starts

Before you work on your business, understand how you decide.

Every decision you make uses both logic and feeling. What differs from person to person is where a decision starts, the sequence in between and what finally settles it. We call that your dominant decision-making sequence. Once you know yours, your partner’s and your team’s, the arguments that felt personal turn out to be two sequences reading the same facts differently. Clashes get resolved, everyone’s decision making improves, and the business runs with less friction.

The Infinite Scaling Methodology

Once you have learnt how to make better decisions, learn how to build a better business.

The lower layers make a business profitable and create systems. The higher layers make it compound. Your membership decides how far up the tower you go.

The problem

Decision Intelligence fixes how you decide. It does not tell you what to build, or in what order. That is where most businesses fail: they are built by trial and error. Tools chosen one at a time, systems that live in one person’s head, products launched before the price and the customer were right, assets never built, and no route to an exit. Every lesson costs money and time, and the next decision is another guess.

The solution

The Infinite Scaling Methodology is the order to build in. Six layers, each one making the next possible, from a profitable foundation to an ecosystem that compounds without you in every decision. Every layer comes with the frameworks that explain it and workbooks written for your decision intelligence sequence, so you build the right thing, in the right order, in the way you decide.

Where the methodology comes from

Hanif Khan started his career designing air traffic control systems for British Aerospace. After the birth of his daughter, when a nine-to-five was no longer an option, he moved into the entrepreneurial world, beginning in property. He is a well-known speaker and mentor in the United Kingdom, known for his 51 creative property plays and for innovative strategies that were first to market. He is also an avid investor who, with Fariza, has helped scale a number of businesses over two decades.

Hanif created the Infinite Scaling Methodology with Fariza as his muse when they launched the Brandscaling Mastermind, to speed up the scaling of the businesses they had invested in and of their mentees. It is the sequence they learned the hard way, built into a done-for-you system so that nobody else has to go through the failures and rebuilds they went through over the last two decades.
