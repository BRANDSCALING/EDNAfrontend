1. Who we are

[Company name], trading as Brandscaling ("Brandscaling", "we", "us"), is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. Company details are in Section 16.

2. Who this policy covers

  • Visitors to our websites.
  • People who take the free E-DNA assessment.
  • Members of any paid tier, and team members added to a business account.
  • People who contact us, apply for Elite or Enterprise, or attend our events.
  • Mentees, affiliates and collaboration partners.

3. What we collect

You give us:

  • Identity and contact: name, email address, telephone number, company name, role, country.
  • Account: sign-in details (passwords are stored hashed and we never see them), two-factor settings, roles in a business account.
  • E-DNA assessment: your answers, your experience band, and the results we calculate from them (core type, subtype, Decision Intelligence Loop results and the other parts of your profile).
  • Content you create: workbook entries, notes, documents, files, images, voice recordings and messages you give to the AI mentors, decision logs.
  • Applications: information in Elite and Enterprise applications, and notes from any live session you attend.
  • Payment: handled by Stripe. We receive a payment token, the card brand, the last four digits, the billing name and address, and the outcome. We never store full card numbers.
  • Correspondence: emails, support requests and complaints.

We collect automatically:

  • Device and usage data: IP address, browser, device type, operating system, pages viewed, features used, time spent, errors and crash reports.
  • AI processing records: the prompts you send to the AI mentors and the responses, so the mentors can remember your context and so we can check quality and safety.
  • Cookies and similar technologies, as described in the Cookie Policy.

We collect from others:

  • Sign-in providers (for example Google or Apple sign-in), if you choose to use them: your name, email address and profile picture.
  • Tools you choose to connect (for example calendar or email). We access only what the connection screen describes, and you can disconnect at any time.
  • Our payment provider and email service, for delivery and fraud records.

We do not want: special category data. The E-DNA assessment does not ask about health, religion, politics, sexuality, ethnicity or similar. E-DNA Part 5 asks about learning preferences and accessibility needs so that we can adapt how content is shown to you; answering is optional, and if you tell us about a disability we treat it as special category data processed with your explicit consent. Please do not enter special category data about yourself or anyone else into the AI mentors or workbooks unless you want us to process it.

5. How we use AI

The AI mentors (AI Architect and AI Alchemist) and the OS Brain process your data to give you responses. How this works:

  • Your AI mentors can see all of your own data in the app, so that their guidance fits you.
  • Your identifiable data is never shown to another member's AI mentor and never used to answer another member.
  • The AI learns across members only from anonymised, aggregated evidence that cannot identify you.
  • Prompts and responses are sent to our AI model providers (listed in the Sub-processor and Data Transfer Register) to generate responses. Our contracts prohibit those providers from using your data to train their models.
  • No decision with legal or similarly significant effects on you is made solely by automated means. E-DNA results are calculated automatically from your answers; they describe decision preferences and do not affect your access, price or rights. You can ask a human to review any AI output.

Full details are in the AI Disclosure and AI Mentor Policy.

6. What other members can see

  • Your E-DNA result is private to you. In a business account, the Owner and Administrators can see the core type of each team member so that the team can work together; they cannot see your assessment answers or detailed report unless you share them.
  • The AI mentors never reveal one member's data to another.

7. Who we share data with

  • Service providers that host, store, process payments, send email, provide AI models and give support. Each is listed, with its role and location, in the Sub-processor and Data Transfer Register. Each acts under a written contract and only on our instructions.
  • Our group companies: Brand Scaling Limited (United Kingdom) and Brandscaling Ai Ltd (United Arab Emirates) run one shared platform and act as joint controllers for it.
  • Our staff and contractors in the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates and Pakistan, under confidentiality obligations and access controls.
  • Other members, only as described in Section 6.
  • Professional advisers, auditors, insurers, regulators, courts and law enforcement, where required.
  • A buyer or successor if we sell or restructure the business; you will be told.

We do not sell personal data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

8. International transfers

Our services are hosted in the United States (Amazon Web Services, us-east-1 region) and we use providers in the United States, the United Kingdom and the European Union.

United Kingdom residents: transfers outside the United Kingdom are protected by the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or by a UK adequacy regulation where one exists (for example for the EU).

DIFC processing: transfers out of the DIFC are made to jurisdictions the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection recognises as adequate, or under DIFC standard contractual clauses or another mechanism permitted by DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020.

You can ask for a copy of the relevant safeguards at info@brandscaling.co.uk.

10. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and role-based permissions, two-factor authentication, logging, regular backups and staff confidentiality obligations. Member data is separated so that one customer's data cannot reach another. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

11. Children

Our services are for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • delete your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to processing, including objecting to direct marketing at any time;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the basis;
  • not be subject to a solely automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects.

Most of these can be done in your account settings, including downloading your E-DNA report and deleting your account. For anything else, email info@brandscaling.co.uk. We respond within one month (UK GDPR) or within the time required by DIFC law, and may ask you to verify your identity.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data you can complain to:

  • United Kingdom: the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk.
  • DIFC: the Commissioner of Data Protection, difc.ae/data-protection.

We would rather resolve it with you first: see our Complaints Procedure.
