Complaints Procedure

Effective: [date]. Last updated: [date].

1. How to complain

Email info@brandscaling.co.uk with your name, the email address on your account, what happened, when, and what you would like us to do. You can also write to the address in the footer.

2. What happens next

StepWhat we doWhen
1. AcknowledgeWe confirm we have received your complaint and give you a referenceWithin 2 working days
2. InvestigateThe Operations Manager reviews your account, records and any AI conversation logs you refer toWithin 10 working days of acknowledgement
3. RespondWe send a written response with our findings and any action, refund or correctionWithin 10 working days of acknowledgement, or we tell you why we need longer and when to expect it
4. ReviewIf you are not satisfied, ask for a review by a co-founderResponse within 15 working days of your request

3. Complaints about data

Complaints about how we handle personal data go to info@brandscaling.co.uk and are handled under this procedure and the Privacy Policy. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (United Kingdom) or the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection (DIFC) at any time.

4. Complaints about an AI mentor response

Use the "Report" control on the message, or email us with the date and time of the conversation. We review the full conversation, correct any error, and change the mentor's instructions where needed.

5. Complaints about another member

Email us with the details. We treat these confidentially, hear both sides, and act under the Acceptable Use Policy.

6. If we cannot resolve it

you may be able to use alternative dispute resolution; we will tell you whether we agree to use a particular scheme. Consumers also keep the right to go to court.

7. Learning from complaints

We log every complaint and review the log quarterly to improve the service.
